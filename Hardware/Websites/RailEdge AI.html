Help and legal
How does RailEdge predict arrival times?

Receivers beside the track detect each train as it passes. Our model combines those timestamps with past delays, speed and congestion, and updates the arrival time every time the train crosses a new point.

How accurate are the predictions?

Estimates sharpen as a train passes more trackside points. They are forecasts, not guarantees, so keep a safety margin for connections.

What does the delay scale mean?

Green means little delay is likely, yellow means the train may or may not run late, and red means a large delay is possible.

Do I need an account?

No. Searching trains needs no sign-up. SMS alerts need only your ticket and a mobile number.

When do SMS alerts start?

At the time of your journey, not before. You can stop them at any time by replying STOP.

Can weather really change my train’s timing?

The weather panel flags conditions on the route ahead, such as fog, heavy rain or storms, that usually lead to speed limits.
