Executive Summary for Judges

The Problem vs. RailEdge AI Solution

Why traditional railway tracking fails in high-density networks and how our solution solves it.

❌ Existing Systems (GPS / Manual Entry)

  • ⚠️ GPS Blackouts: Tunnels, deep cuts, and dense forest corridors cause continuous loss of signal.
  • ⚠️ Manual Station Logging: High human error and delays in station master updates.
  • ⚠️ Static ETA Calculations: Distance/Speed equations ignore track congestion and signal delays ahead.
  • ⚠️ High Infrastructure Cost: Equipping every train coach with active high-maintenance hardware.

✔ RailEdge AI Approach

  • ✅ Zero-Blackout RF Checkpoints: Track-side RFID/LoRa receivers guarantee precise timestamps everywhere.
  • ✅ Fully Automated Data Stream: Zero human intervention; instantaneous signal-post-to-cloud logging.
  • ✅ Dynamic ML Engine: Predicts ETA based on section congestion, historic delays, and train priority.
  • ✅ Low-Cost Passive Track Hardware: Cost-effective, scalable setup for pan-railway deployment.

04 / System Architecture & Feasibility

How RailEdge AI Works at Scale

01

Hardware Layer

Passive trackside RFID/RF receivers powered by solar/signal line tap. Zero moving parts on train engines.

02

Edge Gateway

LoRaWAN / cellular transmission logs arrival timestamps to nearest division server under 200ms latency.

03

AI ML Pipeline

Hosted Python FastAPI + XGBoost backend continuous prediction engine recalculated instantly per event.

04

Display System

REST APIs feed passenger mobile apps, station display boards, and control room dispatchers simultaneously.
